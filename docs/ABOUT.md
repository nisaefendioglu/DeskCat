## Short description (~280 chars)

DeskCat is a tiny macOS menu bar companion. Pip, your desk cat, mirrors your focus on a mini desk scene — writing when you’re in the zone, dozing when you’re idle — and meows once after you’ve been away, so you notice without nagging.

## Medium / Product Hunt style

**Tagline:** A desk cat that notices when you leave.

**Description:**
DeskCat lives in your Mac menu bar. No “Start Focus” button — Pip watches idle time and the frontmost app, then settles into focusing, drowsy, or asleep on a little night desk (lamp, notebook, coffee).

Stay away too long and Pip meows once for that stretch. Come back, leave again, and you’ll hear another. Snooze when you’re intentionally away. English or Turkish. Light, Dark, or System appearance.

Free, open source (MIT). Download the DMG and drag to Applications.
